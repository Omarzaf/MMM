**Strict delta:** No substantive status changed in the monitored interval. PPRA added one visible evaluation record, but it is outside the brief's AI/digital scope. The KP workplace-harassment item is a newly discovered date-only committee follow-up and remains proposed.

- Implementation likelihood remains highest where an item has a notified instrument, named procuring entity, budget line, tender, and public result. Most watched AI/cloud initiatives have not yet crossed all of those gates.
