# 7. What changed / why it matters

- **The only measurable delta is PPRA register volume.** Because counts rose while the visible leading in-scope records did not materially change, the safe conclusion is “register movement, attribution unresolved,” not “new AI procurement.”

- **Implementation evidence remains the dividing line.** AI Hubs and sovereign infrastructure are still procurement-stage; PSW ISO and PRAL VDI are evaluation-stage. None establishes award, delivery, acceptance, certified security, or operational capacity.

- **Data-governance exposure remains contractual and institutional.** Until an enacted horizontal privacy law and final implementation instruments are authenticated, companies and public bodies need explicit project controls for authority, consent, purpose, transfers, retention, security, audit, human review, and redress.

- **Energy is still an unclosed dependency.** Any serious compute or data-centre claim should disclose firm power supply, tariff/PPA, redundant feeds, tested backup runtime, cooling and water design, and the relationship between gross facility power and usable IT load.

# 9. Method and limitations

This is a strict-delta briefing. It compares live evidence after the prior cutoff rather than treating every item published on 31 August as newly occurring in this window. Search indexes, government pages, and procurement registers can be asynchronous, mutable, date-only, cached, or publication-lagged. “No material development authenticated” therefore means no qualifying change was evidenced by the final checkpoint; it is not proof that no institution acted privately. Confidence levels measure evidence quality, not importance.
