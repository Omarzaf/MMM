**Personal-data-protection legislation:** MoITT told the National Assembly in May that it had finalized a bill, sometimes calling it the “Personal Data Protection Act 2026.” No current bill text, parliamentary passage, presidential assent, or Gazette publication was located. **Status: ministry-finalized proposal, not enacted. No new compliance deadline, audit order, enforcement circular, or legal notification was found. **Status: approved cybersecurity framework, not verified as a generally enforceable statute. Confidence: medium-high.**

## **7\. What Changed / Why It Matters**

The strict post-cutoff answer is: **nothing material changed**.

The newly discovered Punjab records are nevertheless significant because they show concrete provincial demand for GPU capacity, AI-ready data-centre infrastructure, and a government AI-cloud design.
