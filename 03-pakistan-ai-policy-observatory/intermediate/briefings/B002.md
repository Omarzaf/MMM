## **2\. Policy, Regulation, and Governance**

**Development:** No new AI legislation, strategy approval, regulation, standard, consultation, or institutional decision was verified.

**Status:** Unchanged. The Senate record says the Regulation of Artificial Intelligence Bill 2024 was neither passed nor rejected by committee and consequently stands withdrawn and disposed of. It therefore has no enacted legal effect.

**Evidence:** [Senate bill record](https://www.senate.gov.pk/en/billsummary.php?bid=1544), originally introduced 9 September 2024; current record accessed 20 August 2026\.

**What changed:** Nothing during this window.

**Why it matters:** References to this bill as Pakistan’s enacted “AI law” remain inaccurate. Any binding AI-governance framework would require fresh legislation or another valid regulatory instrument.

**Confidence:** High.
