II.

How far anything got

Every record sits somewhere between an intention and a working system. Placing all 52 on the same ladder makes the shape of the period visible at once: the field is wide at the bottom and thin at the top, and the evidence thins in the opposite direction to the claims.

On the word “operating”Six records claim a facility or system is running. Not one of them carries a high-confidence rating. That gap is the finding, not a footnote to it.

The inversion

The 6 published rules are all high confidence: a notified regulation is a document you can open. The 6 records claiming a facility or system is running are all medium: an inauguration, a launch claim or an authority’s own report of operation is not premises-level, load-level or assurance-level evidence. The strongest claims rest on the weakest record.

This is not an accusation of overstatement. It is a measurement problem. Rules publish themselves; operation does not. Until utilisation, accepted load, staffing or independent assurance is disclosed, the top rung stays medium no matter how many ribbons are cut.

What each rung stands on

The inversion has a mechanism, and the source register shows it. A rule is its own evidence: the gazette or register that notifies it is the record. A claim of operation arrives as a statement — a release, a broadcast, a company report — and nothing in the ledger sits beneath it. Place each record on the ring of the strongest primary source it cites, and the rungs come apart.

Eleven procurements, none past evaluation

Procurement is the busiest route from a policy to a working system, and in this window it stops at one gate. Two procedures are still open, seven have closed to bids and two are in evaluation. Not one reached award. Everything a reader might picture as capacity — contracts, deliveries, accepted systems — lies on the far side.

III.

The window we could see

The evidence base is extensive; the monitoring cadence is not. Both facts have to travel together, because the second one bounds every claim the first one can support.

On absenceThe corpus covers 0.98% of the observed span, in 14 bounded windows. Every inter-briefing gap exceeded 30 minutes. A quiet day in the briefings is not a quiet day in the country.

A timeline shows when things happened. It cannot show when anyone was looking. Wound into a clock — one turn per day, midnight at the top — the 14 briefing windows collapse into a narrow beam: 9 of them opened between 13:30 and 15:30 UTC, early evening in Pakistan. Anything that happened outside that beam reached this record only through the later verification search, which is how the 1 September cyber MoU was found.

VII.

Method and limits

The briefing corpus was a discovery index, never independent corroboration. Confidence attaches to the stated claim, not to eventual delivery.

High

Direct, accessible primary record supports the event and lifecycle status; material contradictions are resolved or explicitly bounded.

Medium

Core event is supported, but the operative instrument, implementation evidence, independent corroboration or important particulars are missing.

Low

Only an indirect, secondary, inaccessible or contradictory record supports the claim; no provision-level or implementation conclusion is drawn.

Lifecycle before language

Proposal is not approval; tender is not award; MoU is not commissioning; inauguration is not verified load or use.

Source hierarchy

Operative law and official records lead, then direct institutional publications, then credible reporting. Repeated handouts are not independent corroboration.

Confidence is scoped

High, Medium and Low attach to the stated event and lifecycle claim — not to future execution, impact or completeness.

Coverage is sparse

14 units against 1,440 nominal intervals. Missing intervals were documented and never imputed as “no change.”

Contradictions stay visible

Mutable dates, inaccessible instruments and conflicting trackers remain unresolved rather than silently harmonised.

An agent can open 111 sources, hold a lifecycle vocabulary steady across 52 records and refuse to let a handout count twice.
