# HipHop Pakistan — Live Research Brief

Date checked: 23 August 2026  
Status: Planning-level source verification complete; links, figures, and image licenses require a publication recheck  
Purpose: Define the defensible public narrative, graph inputs, case pathways, hyperlinks, and image-rights boundaries for the editorial product.

## 1. Editorial conclusion

Pakistan experienced a distribution revolution between 2014 and 2026. Mobile broadband, the restoration of YouTube, local streaming experiments, global audio platforms, lower-cost production tools, and international label partnerships expanded the routes through which music could be made visible.

That change is real, but it is not the same as a measured industry boom. Public evidence remains much stronger for connectivity, platform attention, and exceptional success stories than for artist income, contracts, royalties, employment, venue economics, or the distribution of value.

The product's governing interpretation to test is therefore:

> Pakistan can now measure reach much better than livelihood.

This is an editorial synthesis of the reviewed public record, not a computed index or completed empirical finding. The published version must qualify it on first use and must not turn it into a causal graph comparing unlike measures.

Every chapter must preserve the distinction between:

1. national infrastructure;
2. activity reported by one platform or company;
3. small purposive livelihood samples;
4. individual artist or institutional cases; and
5. findings that still require the planned fieldwork.

## 2. Source classes and visual labels

| Label | Meaning | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official series | Government or regulator statistics with stated dates and units | National infrastructure context, with revision and endpoint notes |
| Platform-reported | A platform's own analytics or campaign figures | Evidence about that platform only; no national-market or income inference |
| Purposive sample | A non-random study with named sample size and coverage | Descriptive sample snapshot; never a population estimate |
| Case evidence | A documented artist, scene, company, or policy pathway | Mechanism illustration; never an industry-wide denominator |
| Research target | A quantity or relationship the planned study intends to collect | Methods and evidence-gap visuals only |

Every figure must visibly carry one of these labels.

## 3. Historical spine and hyperlinks

### 2014 — the access break

Pakistan's 3G/4G spectrum auction supplies the infrastructure starting point. The April 2014 auction raised approximately US$1.1 billion, but the relevant analytical consequence is the rapid expansion of mobile broadband rather than the auction receipt itself.

Primary and contemporaneous links:

- [Dawn / Reuters — spectrum-auction result](https://www.dawn.com/news/1101760/11-billion-raised-from-3g-4g-auction)
- [Pakistan Economic Survey 2021–22 — historical broadband table](https://www.finance.gov.pk/survey/chapter_22/Economic%20Survey%202021-22.pdf)
- [Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26 — current connectivity series](https://finance.gov.pk/survey/chapter_26/15_Information_Technology.pdf)

### 2015 — local streaming experiments

Taazi and Patari attempted to turn Pakistani catalogues into legal digital-distribution businesses. Launch, catalogue, royalty-split, funding, and payout figures were reported by the platforms or their founders and must remain attributed claims. Later payment disputes make the episode a useful illustration of the gap between audience building and reliable settlement.

Links:

- [The Express Tribune — Taazi launch](https://tribune.com.pk/story/934573/time-for-taazis-baazi)
- [The Express Tribune — Patari public launch](https://tribune.com.pk/story/950790/patari-opens-for-public)
- [Dawn — Patari seed funding](https://www.dawn.com/news/1305568/music-streaming-service-patari-raises-200000-in-seed-investment)
- [The Express Tribune — reported payment disputes](https://tribune.com.pk/story/1945908/patari-hot-water-musicians-complain-payment-issues)

### 2016 — YouTube returns

YouTube returned in January 2016 after a multi-year block through a localized service. Restoration reopened a central distribution channel while also showing that platform access and state control developed together.

Links:

- [The Express Tribune — restoration after three years](https://tribune.com.pk/story/1030136/youtube-ban-lifted-after-three-years)
- [Digital Rights Foundation — governance implications](https://digitalrightsfoundation.pk/youtubepk2016/)

### 2016–19 — visibility grows, local economics remain unstable

The number of Pakistan-based YouTube channels above 100,000 subscribers rose sharply across all creator categories. Patari announced artist payouts and investment while musicians later alleged delayed payment. These facts support a distribution-change chapter, not a music-revenue series.

Links:

- [Google — Pakistan creator snapshot](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/6956/TwG_APAC_Infographic_Pakistan_WEB_C1_5X913nr.pdf)
- [Dawn — follow-up on Patari's institutional difficulties](https://www.dawn.com/news/1485993/can-patari-get-back-on-its-feet-again)

### 2020 — live shock, intensified home production

COVID-19 disrupted concerts, weddings, and venue work while increasing the visibility of remote and home-based production. Home production existed before the pandemic, so the chapter must describe acceleration and exposure, not invention.

Links:

- [Arab News — reported live-income disruption](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1702076/pakistan)
- [Dawn — Abdullah Siddiqui's room-made video](https://www.dawn.com/news/1576012)
- [Dawn Images — Coke Studio 2020 home-studio production](https://images.dawn.com/news/1186087/an-exclusive-preview-of-coke-studio-season-2020-through-the-words-of-its-maestro-rohail-hyatt)

### 2021 — Spotify enters

Spotify launched in Pakistan as part of an expansion into more than 80 markets. This adds a global audio intermediary and playlist system; it does not establish local subscriber totals, payout levels, or equal editorial access.

Links:

- [Spotify — international expansion announcement](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2021-02-22/spotify-expands-international-footprint-bringing-audio-to-80-new-markets/)
- [Business Recorder — Pakistan availability](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40067096)

### 2022 — globally legible breakthroughs

Arooj Aftab became the first Pakistani woman to win a Grammy, while “Pasoori” became Spotify's most-streamed Pakistani song of 2022. These are important export and symbolic-prestige cases, not measures of average livelihood.

Links:

- [Recording Academy — Arooj Aftab's Grammy](https://www.grammy.com/news/arooj-aftab-grammy-win-history-first-pakistani-woman-mohabbat-vulture-prince-video-run-the-world/)
- [The Guardian — “Pasoori” and global circulation](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2022/may/13/global-hit-pasoori-opens-doors-for-pakistani-pop)
- [Spotify — platform account of “Pasoori”](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2023-02-15/pakistani-artist-shae-gill-breaks-streaming-records-with-viral-hit-pasoori/)

### 2023–24 — platform programs and global rights-holders

Spotify moved from availability toward active market-making through EQUAL, RADAR, Fresh Finds, masterclasses, and local playlists. UMG, Sony, Mass Appeal, and Warner announced catalogue, repertoire, or artist partnerships touching Pakistani music and its diaspora. Contract terms, rights splits, and broad employment effects remain unavailable.

Links:

- [Spotify — Pakistan programs and masterclass](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2023-04-06/spotify-turns-up-the-volume-in-pakistan-with-events-and-music-campaigns/)
- [Warner Music — Giraffe Pakistan partnership](https://www.wmg.com/news/warner-music-partners-with-leading-pakistani-music-company-giraffe)
- [Music Business Worldwide — Mass Appeal partnerships](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/nass-mass-appeal-expands-into-pakistan-partners-with-two-of-the-countrys-biggest-artists-talha-anjum-and-umair/)

### 2023–26 — policy catches up incompletely

The government-hosted National Music Policy document identifies weak copyright enforcement, fragmented royalty collection, missing archives, and limited infrastructure. It proposes registration, licensing, welfare, education, archive, and enforcement mechanisms. The document calls itself a final draft, has 2024 PDF metadata, and does not provide a clear commencement date, implementation timetable, or budget. A 2025 UK review still describes it as a draft.

Links:

- [Government-hosted National Music Policy PDF](https://epwing.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Music%20Policy%20final.pdf)
- [Radio Pakistan — 2023 policy announcement](https://www.radio.gov.pk/26-06-2023/pakistans-first-music-policy-prepared-to-resolve-issues-of-piracy-copyrights-marriyum)
- [WIPO Lex — Copyright Ordinance](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/3482)
- [UK Government — 2025 review of Pakistan's IP regime](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/review-of-pakistan-ip-regime/review-of-pakistan-ip-regime)

### 2025–26 — wider access, unresolved value distribution

Official figures show continuing broadband expansion. Spotify's five-year Pakistan release reports large growth in listening, Pakistani-artist streams, playlists, and represented artists. Those Spotify figures are valuable as a platform pulse but have no absolute listener, stream, or artist denominators and do not reveal payments.

Links:

- [Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26](https://finance.gov.pk/survey/chapter_26/15_Information_Technology.pdf)
- [Spotify — five years in Pakistan](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-04-21/5-years-pakistan-music-trends/)

### 2026 epilogue — 5G begins

The March 2026 spectrum auction and selected-city service launches belong in an epilogue. They establish a new infrastructure cycle, not nationwide adoption or a music-sector effect.

Links:

- [Ministry of IT and Telecommunication — auction opening](https://www.moitt.gov.pk/NewsDetail/Yzk3N2RhMzYtMGNkMS00OTI5LWFhNGMtOGQ1NzgzYzFmZmZi)

## 5. Case pathways

### Lyari Underground / LUG Sounds

Use the pathway `local collective → branded/platform visibility → loss of space and disruption → self-built production node`. This case shows that visibility did not remove the infrastructure problem.

- [Dawn Images — LUG studio profile](https://images.dawn.com/news/1193312/how-lyari-underground-built-a-studio-from-scratch-in-karachis-most-resilient-neighbourhood)
- [Dawn / Reuters — Lyari Underground and Patari Tabeer](https://www.dawn.com/news/1313662)

Unknown: audited revenue, contract terms, ownership, number of artists served, and current continuity.

### Eva B

Use the pathway `online and local-studio practice → independent label development → Coke Studio amplification → international placement`. The argument is that the local artist and production network existed before the branded breakthrough.

- [Dawn / AFP — Eva B profile](https://images.dawn.com/news/1190055)
- [The National — Eva B and “Rozi”](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/music/2022/06/15/pakistani-ms-marvel-rapper-eva-b-im-more-than-just-my-niqab/)

Unknown: master and composition ownership, fees, royalties, label split, and long-term income. Do not call her definitively Pakistan's first female rapper.

### Abid Brohi / Patari Tabeer

Use the pathway `regional performance → local discovery → metropolitan producer pairing → digital release`. This demonstrates discovery across geographic and language boundaries without proving a durable career.

- [The News — discovery pathway](https://www.thenews.com.pk/magazine/instep-today/179654-From-the-streets-to-the-studio-the-story-of-Abid-Brohi)
- [Dawn / Reuters — Tabeer model](https://www.dawn.com/news/1313662)

Unknown: contract terms, release income, royalty accounting, follow-on opportunities, and current career continuity.

### Shae Gill and “Pasoori”

Use the pathway `bedroom covers → Coke Studio invitation → global platform circulation → later rights distinction`. Visibility, performer participation, and control over a song must remain separate variables.

- [Spotify — platform profile](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2023-02-15/pakistani-artist-shae-gill-breaks-streaming-records-with-viral-hit-pasoori/)
- [Hindustan Times — Gill on remake control](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/bollywood/pasoori-singer-shae-gill-on-satyaprem-ki-katha-song-bollywood-remake-101687955005470.html)

Unknown: performance fee, royalty flows, ownership shares, contract terms, and how the breakthrough changed long-term music income.

### Young Stunners, Talha Anjum, and Umair

Use the pathway `peer crew and file-sharing circulation → small live circuit → platform visibility → international label partnership`. Local teams and long-term persistence preceded formal international entry.

- [Dawn — career and live-circuit interview](https://www.dawn.com/news/1887779/the-icon-interview)
- [Spotify — narrowly scoped Gen Z listening window](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2023-09-15/what-gen-z-listeners-are-streaming-in-pakistan/)
- [Music Business Worldwide — Mass Appeal partnerships](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/nass-mass-appeal-expands-into-pakistan-partners-with-two-of-the-countrys-biggest-artists-talha-anjum-and-umair/)

Unknown: deal structure, advances, ownership, royalties, audience geography, and scene-wide spillover.

### Abdullah Siddiqui

Use the pathway `home production → festival and radio intermediaries → branded stage → production leadership`. Low-cost software lowered one threshold, but intermediaries remained decisive.

- [Forbes — career chronology](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sonyarehman/2024/04/08/the-influential-voice-charting-a-new-course-in-pakistani-music/)
- [Dawn — room-made production during the 2020 shock](https://www.dawn.com/news/1576012)

Unknown: ownership and fee structures across roles, the relative value of each intermediary, and whether the pathway is reproducible for less-connected producers.

### Arooj Aftab

Use the diaspora pathway to distinguish Pakistani-rooted cultural recognition from the domestic location of institutions, financing, and revenue.

- [Recording Academy — Grammy account](https://www.grammy.com/news/arooj-aftab-grammy-win-history-first-pakistani-woman-mohabbat-vulture-prince-video-run-the-world/)
- [Recording Academy — artist record](https://www.grammy.com/artists/arooj-aftab/37873/)
- [Al Jazeera — independent account of the 2022 award](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/4/4/arooj-aftab-becomes-first-pakistani-singer-to-win-a-grammy)

Unknown: the share of audience and revenue located in Pakistan, domestic institutional spillover, rights and financing structures, and whether symbolic recognition produced durable local investment.

A contextual image cannot stand in as documentary evidence of a named case. When an open-license venue, city, telecommunications, or regional-music photograph is topically adjacent rather than directly documentary, label it `Context photograph`, caption only what it visibly depicts, and state that it does not portray the named artist or event.

For contemporary artists without licensed photography, use one of three treatments:

1. a text-led case panel with direct source links;
2. a platform-compliant external link without copying its thumbnail; or
3. an original editorial illustration labeled as illustration and containing no fabricated documentary details.

Per-image metadata must include creator, file page, original URL, date, license, license URL, modifications, and rights-check date. Cropped or graded share-alike assets must disclose the adaptation and retain compatible licensing.

## 7. Claims to exclude

- No national music-industry revenue, employment, artist-income, royalty, or contract-prevalence estimate.
- No inference that streams or views equal artist payment.
- No use of global or regional IFPI totals as a Pakistan estimate.
- No claim that 2020 invented home production.
- No claim that the National Music Policy is fully implemented.
- No claim that label partnerships reveal better contractual terms.
- No generalization from Spotify to the whole listening market.
- No generalization from the BNU/UNESCO purposive music sample to Pakistan's entire music workforce.
- No claim that the 360-track or 80–100-artist research targets are completed datasets.
- No unlicensed contemporary artist portraits.

## 8. Research status for implementation

The current evidence is sufficient to write and visualize:

- a sourced infrastructure history;
- a platformization chapter;
- a cautiously quantitative livelihood section using small sample snapshots;
- a rights and policy gap section;
- seven human case pathways;
- an evidence-quality and missing-data chapter; and
- a transparent conclusion about the divergence between visibility and industrial maturity.

The evidence is not sufficient to publish a national performance ranking or a definitive verdict on industry-wide growth. The site should present that absence as a central analytical finding rather than hiding it behind decorative charts.
